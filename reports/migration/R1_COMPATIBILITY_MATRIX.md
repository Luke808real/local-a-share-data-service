## Explicit unknowns and conflict notes

1. `index_bars.amount` has no index-specific unit declaration at the pinned source; it remains `UNKNOWN` rather than inheriting the stock-bar amount unit by assumption.
2. Schema support for `adj_factors.adjust_type=qfq` does not prove that a default-configured lake contains QFQ factors; the packaged default is HFQ only.
3. `trading_status` has a material source/semantics conflict across registry, executable step, and catalog. This document preserves the three statements and makes no contract-selection or compatibility decision.
